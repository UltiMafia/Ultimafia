   under-calls terse accused forms badly (B1) — the highest-value fix in the list.

3. **`sue me`** — the rubric lists it under both VIOLATES and ALLOWED (A4).
4. **Moderation requests** — is `report them` / `can we report X` / `just ban his ass` OGI (B2)?
5. **Targeting-test enforcement** — no policy change; the judge ignores four carve-outs it
   already has (A3), and under-calls person-directed insults (B3, B4).
6. **P&A is inconsistent, not too strict** — group A argues for relaxation, groups B3/B4 argue
   against. This needs a single rule that produces both outcomes, not a dial.
